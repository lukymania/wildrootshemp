## SECTION 4: KEY FINDINGS

### 4.1 NANO-HEMP Pricing Range
- **Lowest nano-hemp price/MG:** CBD Living 10,000mg at **$0.020/mg**
- **Highest nano-hemp price/MG:** Quicksilver Scientific at **$0.143-$0.158/mg**
- **Typical nano-hemp range:** $0.033 - $0.080/mg for mid-to-high potency
- **Entry-level nano-hemp (300-750mg):** $0.060 - $0.117/mg

### 4.2 Retailer Landscape
- **Walmart:** Does NOT carry nano-hemp products. Limited hemp extract; mostly hemp seed oil (food grade).
- **Whole Foods:** Does NOT carry nano-hemp tinctures. Stocks Manitoba Harvest hemp seed oil only (food grade).
- **Sprouts:** Carries NuLeaf Naturals (non-nano full spectrum) in Colorado. No nano-hemp tinctures found at Sprouts.
- **Nano-hemp is primarily sold DTC (direct-to-consumer) online**, NOT at major brick-and-mortar retailers.

### 4.3 Wild Roots Competitive Positioning
Based on this data, Wild Roots nano-hemp products compete in a market where:
- Most nano-hemp brands sell DTC online (not at major retailers)
- Price/MG for nano products ranges from $0.020 (bulk) to $0.160 (premium/liposomal)
- Mid-range nano brands (Pure Craft, CBD Living, Nanocraft) price at $0.033-$0.067/mg
- Premium liposomal nano (Quicksilver Scientific) commands $0.14+/mg
- Non-nano full spectrum tinctures at Sprouts (NuLeaf) price at $0.042-$0.054/mg
- Major retailers (Walmart, Whole Foods) are NOT yet stocking nano-hemp wellness tinctures

### 4.4 Key Gaps in the Market
1. **No major brick-and-mortar retailer** (Walmart, Whole Foods, Sprouts) currently stocks nano-hemp extract tinctures
2. **Sprouts is the only retailer** carrying high-MG hemp extract (NuLeaf, non-nano)
3. Nano-hemp remains a **DTC-online dominated category**
4. Price/MG varies by 8x between lowest (CBD Living bulk) and highest (Quicksilver)
