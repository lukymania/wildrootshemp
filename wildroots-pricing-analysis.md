## Key Findings

### 🏆 Nano-Hemp Competitors (Most Direct Competition)
1. **CBD American Shaman** — The most established nano-hemp competitor. Pricing slightly below Wild Roots on most products. Available on Amazon.
2. **CBD Living** — Strong nano product line at significantly lower prices. Direct competition on sprays and pet products.
3. **CBD Living Freeze/Topicals** — Nano roll-on at $0.14/MG vs. Wild Roots' $0.45/MG.
4. **Empe USA** — Nano tinctures at competitive pricing.

### 💡 Pricing Insights
- **Daily Roots** ($0.217/MG) and **Paw & Root** ($0.217/MG) are well-positioned within the nano-enhanced range.
- **Deep Roots** ($0.183/MG) is competitive but at the high end of nano pricing.
- **Deep Rest** ($0.533/MG) is the most vulnerable — highest $/MG across all categories. The 150MG total content may be perceived as low for an $80 price point. Consider either increasing MG content or highlighting the CBN premium more prominently.
- **Root Relief** ($0.450/MG) is the biggest pricing risk — smallest product (100MG) with the highest per-MG cost among topicals. Competitors offer 250-500MG at lower $/MG.

### 📊 Market Context
- Non-nano hemp oils sell for $0.05–$0.10/MG (Lazarus, CBDistillery, Extract Labs)
- Premium non-nano (Charlotte's Web, Lord Jones) sells for $0.20–$0.25/MG
- Nano-enhanced products command a 2-3x premium over standard oils
- Wild Roots' nano pricing is **justified** but requires strong education about nano-technology value
