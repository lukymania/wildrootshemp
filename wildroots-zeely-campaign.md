**1. Product Photos Matter Most**
- Use the clean product shots on white/cream backgrounds
- Full product view, not cropped
- Neutral backgrounds without clutter
- High resolution (the AI needs detail to work with)

**2. Brand DNA Always ON**
- For every creative, keep Brand DNA enabled
- This ensures consistent colors (forest green, terra cotta, cream)
- Fonts will match (Playfair Display + Lato)

**3. The "No Cannabis Look" Rule**
- In every prompt, include: "NO cannabis leaves, NO marijuana imagery, NO psychedelic colors, NO stereotypical hemp aesthetics"

- Use terms: "wellness," "natural," "organic," "premium," "family" — never "CBD," "cannabis," "cannabinoid," "THC"

**4. A/B Testing Framework**
- Run 3-5 creatives per campaign simultaneously
- After 3 days, kill the lowest CTR
- After 7 days, scale the winner by 50% budget
- Generate 2 new variations of the winner for the next test

- Cold traffic (awareness): $15-20/day per campaign
- Warm retargeting: $10-15/day per campaign
- Viral/organic: $0 (just scheduling)
