Q&A

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about nano CBD, our rankings, and how to choose the right product.

What is nano CBD and how does it work? +

Nano CBD is CBD that has been broken down into nanoparticles, typically under 100 nanometers in size. This process dramatically increases the surface area of the CBD molecules, allowing for significantly higher absorption rates (bioavailability) when consumed. Standard CBD oil has a bioavailability of only 6-10%, while nano CBD can achieve 80-90%+ bioavailability, meaning your body absorbs far more of the active compound.

How much more effective is nano CBD compared to regular CBD? +

Research indicates that nano CBD is approximately 10-20 times more bioavailable than standard CBD oil. While regular CBD oil offers only 6-10% absorption, nano CBD formulations like Arkos Bioscience achieve 90%+ bioavailability. This means a 10mg dose of nano CBD could deliver the same effects as 100mg or more of standard CBD oil.

What particle size should I look for in nano CBD? +

For optimal absorption, look for nano CBD with particle sizes under 100nm. The best products, like Arkos Bioscience, achieve particle sizes below 60nm. Smaller particles generally offer better bioavailability, but the delivery technology and formulation quality also play critical roles in effectiveness.

Are nano CBD products THC-free? +

Most high-quality nano CBD products are THC-free or contain only trace amounts below 0.3%. Products like Arkos Bioscience guarantee 0% THC through rigorous third-party lab testing. Always check the Certificate of Analysis (COA) to verify THC content before purchasing any CBD product.

How do you test and rank nano CBD products? +

Our ranking methodology evaluates products across five weighted categories: Bioavailability Score (30%), Third-Party Testing (25%), Formulation Quality (20%), Value for Money (15%), and Customer Satisfaction (10%). We review independent lab reports, analyze particle size claims, assess manufacturing standards, and aggregate verified customer reviews to produce our comprehensive rankings.

Is nano CBD safe to use? +

Yes, nano CBD from reputable manufacturers is generally considered safe. The proprietary nano fragmentation technology process uses FDA-approved food-grade ingredients. However, always purchase from brands that provide third-party lab testing (COAs) to verify purity and safety. Our top-ranked brands all meet strict safety and quality standards. As with any supplement, consult your healthcare provider before use.

How quickly does nano CBD take effect? +

One of the major advantages of nano CBD is its rapid onset time. Most users report feeling effects within 10-15 minutes, compared to 45-90 minutes for traditional CBD oil. This is because nano particles bypass much of the digestive process and are absorbed directly through the mucous membranes in the mouth and stomach.

Can I add nano CBD to my drinks? +

Absolutely! One of the biggest advantages of nano CBD is that it's water-soluble. Unlike traditional CBD which separates and floats, nano CBD dissolves cleanly into any beverage — coffee, tea, smoothies, or just water. This makes dosing far more convenient and consistent.

How are the nano CBD rankings determined? +

Our rankings use a weighted 5-factor scoring methodology developed over 6 weeks of testing. Bioavailability accounts for 30% of the score — we analyze published pharmacokinetic data and conduct comparative dissolution testing to measure how much CBD actually enters circulation. Third-party lab verification is 25% — every product must have current, independent COAs verifying potency, purity, and contaminant screening. Formulation quality is 20% — we evaluate particle size data (under 60nm is ideal), nano-emulsion stability, and the scientific credentials of the formulation team. Value analysis is 15% — we calculate the true cost per effective milligram using bioavailability-adjusted pricing, not just the sticker price. Customer satisfaction rounds out the final 10% from verified user feedback. No manufacturer input or sponsorship influences the scores.
