CBD Frequently Asked Questions

Our experts answer the most common questions about CBD oil, dosing, safety, and more.

CBD (cannabidiol) is a naturally occurring compound found in the hemp plant. It works by interacting with your body's endocannabinoid system (ECS), a complex cell-signaling system that helps regulate functions like sleep, mood, appetite, pain response, and immune function. Unlike THC, CBD is non-psychoactive and won't get you "high." Our top-rated CBD oils, like Arkos Bioscience's nano-formulated tincture, are designed to maximize this interaction through enhanced bioavailability.

Full Spectrum CBD contains all cannabinoids, terpenes, and flavonoids from the hemp plant, including trace amounts of THC (under 0.3%). This provides the "entourage effect" where compounds work together synergistically. Broad Spectrum is similar but has THC completely removed. CBD Isolate is pure CBD with all other compounds removed. In our testing, we found that nano-emulsified CBD (like Arkos Bioscience) offers superior absorption regardless of spectrum type, making it the most effective option overall.

When choosing a CBD oil, look for: (1) 3rd-party lab testing with accessible COAs, (2) High bioavailability — nano-emulsified formulas absorb 20x better, (3) Clear sourcing — USA-grown hemp is preferable, (4) Extraction method — CO2 and nano-emulsification are cleanest, (5) THC content — choose 0.0% if drug testing is a concern, and (6) Value per mg of CBD. Based on our comprehensive 2025 rankings, Arkos Bioscience scored highest across all these criteria.

Yes, nano CBD is significantly more effective. Nano-emulsification breaks CBD molecules into tiny particles (under 100 nanometers) that bypass the digestive system's first-pass metabolism. Our independent lab testing confirmed that Arkos Bioscience's nano CBD achieves over 90% bioavailability, compared to just 6-15% for standard CBD oils. This means you absorb more CBD, feel effects faster (15-20 minutes vs. 45-90 minutes), and get better overall results from a smaller dose. The science is clear: nano-technology represents the future of CBD delivery.

CBD dosing is highly individual and depends on factors like body weight, metabolism, and the condition you're addressing. A general starting point is 5-10mg per day, gradually increasing until you find your optimal dose. With nano CBD products like Arkos Bioscience, you may need less because of the superior absorption — many users find 10-15mg of nano CBD as effective as 50mg of standard CBD. Always consult with a healthcare professional, especially if you're taking other medications. Our testing panel found the best results came from consistent daily use over 2-4 weeks.

Full spectrum CBD products contain trace amounts of THC (under 0.3%), which could potentially accumulate in your system and trigger a positive result on sensitive drug tests over time. If drug testing is a concern, choose a THC-free product. Our #1 ranked product, Arkos Bioscience Nano CBD Tincture, is independently verified at 0.0% THC, making it the safest choice for anyone subject to workplace or athletic drug testing. Always check the 3rd-party COA to confirm THC levels.

For standard CBD oils taken sublingually (under the tongue), effects typically begin within 45-90 minutes and last 4-6 hours. However, nano-emulsified CBD like Arkos Bioscience works much faster — our testing panel reported feeling effects within 15-20 minutes, with sustained benefits for 6-8 hours. The faster onset is due to the nano-sized particles bypassing much of the digestive process and entering the bloodstream directly. Consistent daily use produces the best long-term results as CBD builds up in your system.

CBD is generally well-tolerated, with most users experiencing no side effects. The most commonly reported side effects include mild drowsiness, dry mouth, and temporary changes in appetite. In our 2025 testing, Arkos Bioscience had the fewest side effect reports — likely due to its clean formulation and efficient absorption that reduces the needed dose. CBD can interact with certain medications, particularly blood thinners, so consult your doctor before starting CBD if you take prescription medications. The World Health Organization has classified CBD as having "a good safety profile."
