By Wild Roots Hemp · July 12, 2026 · 6 min read

After a grueling training session, your muscles are screaming, your joints feel stiff, and the idea of doing it all again tomorrow seems almost impossible. Understanding Exercise-Induced Inflammation

When you push your body through intense training, microscopic damage occurs in muscle fibers. This triggers an inflammatory response, a natural and necessary process that leads to muscle adaptation and growth. However, excessive or prolonged inflammation can result in delayed onset muscle soreness (DOMS), reduced range of motion, and longer recovery times between sessions.

The key is not eliminating inflammation entirely, but supporting the body's natural ability to manage it efficiently. This is where hemp enters the conversation. Hemp compounds interact with the body's endocannabinoid system, a regulatory network involved in inflammation response, pain perception, and homeostasis.
