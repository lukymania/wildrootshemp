Product FAQ

Common Questions About Paw & Root

Everything you need to know before ordering for your pet.

Is this safe for all pets?

Paw & Root is formulated for dogs and cats of all breeds and sizes. Our gentle, human-grade formula contains only four simple ingredients: Nano-Hemp, Distilled Water, Vegetable Glycerin, and Propylene Glycol. If your pet has a pre-existing condition or is on medication, consult your veterinarian before use.

How much should I give my pet?

Each serving contains 10MG of nano-hemp. We recommend starting with a half serving for small pets (under 25 lbs) and a full serving for larger pets. Adjust as needed based on your pet's size and response. The included dropper makes measuring easy and precise.

How do I administer it?

Two easy options: squeeze one full dropper into your pet's water bowl and let them drink naturally, or place the desired amount directly into their mouth. Most pets accept it readily due to the neutral, gentle formulation. For best results, use consistently as part of your pet's daily routine.

Can my pet have too much?

Our product is formulated with a safe, measured 10MG per serving. While hemp is generally well-tolerated by pets, we recommend following the suggested serving size and keeping the bottle stored safely out of reach. If you suspect your pet has consumed an excessive amount, contact your veterinarian.
