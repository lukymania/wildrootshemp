The Mammalian Endocannabinoid System

Every mammal on earth has an endocannabinoid system (ECS), a complex network of receptors that helps regulate mood, stress response, sleep, and overall balance. Dogs, cats, horses, and humans all share this ancient biological system. When the ECS is supported, the body can better manage everyday stressors that might otherwise trigger anxious behaviors.

For pets, environmental stressors can be overwhelming. Loud noises like fireworks or construction, changes in routine, separation from their humans, or visits to the vet can all activate a stress response that leaves your pet panting, pacing, or hiding for hours. Hemp compounds interact with the ECS in a way that may help promote a sense of calm without sedation.

Signs Your Pet May Benefit from Hemp

  • Excessive pacing or panting during storms or fireworks
  • Hiding, trembling, or destructive behavior when left alone
  • Over-grooming or obsessive licking
  • Reluctance to enter vehicles or visit the veterinarian
  • General nervousness in new environments

Introducing Hemp to Your Pet

Start low and go slow. Begin with the smallest recommended serving based on your pet's weight and observe their behavior for several days before making any adjustments. Consistency is key. Hemp works best when given regularly, allowing the compounds to build up in your pet's system over time.

Many pet owners find that giving hemp 30 to 45 minutes before a known stressor, like a thunderstorm or car ride, provides the most noticeable benefit. For ongoing anxiety, a consistent daily routine tends to yield the best results.
